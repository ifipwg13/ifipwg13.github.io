How it works

  1. Register: add 👍 to the registration Issue’s opening post. Your GitHub account identifies your registration.
  2. Withdraw: click your selected 👍 again to remove it.
  3. Propose a talk: when submission is open, enter a title, authors, and abstract. Proposing a talk does not register you to attend.
  4. Edit or discuss: open a proposal from the talk list. Edit your own proposal or comment on another proposal. To withdraw your talk, comment “Withdrawn” and close it.

Participant accounts and proposal contents stay in the private meeting area.
