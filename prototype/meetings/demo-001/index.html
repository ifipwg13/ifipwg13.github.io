How it works once the meeting is open

  1. Register: open the participants page and add 👍 to the opening post. Your GitHub account identifies your registration. Inspect that reaction to see registered accounts.
  2. Withdraw: click your selected 👍 again to remove it. You can register again while registration is open.
  3. Propose a talk: enter a title, authors, and abstract in the submission form. You may propose more than one talk, or attend without proposing one.
  4. Edit or discuss: open a proposal from the talk list. You can edit your own proposal and comment on other proposals. To withdraw your proposal, add a comment saying “Withdrawn” and close it.

Participant accounts and proposal contents stay in the private GitHub meeting area. They are not displayed on this website.
